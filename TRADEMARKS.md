## Nature of Use

The icons included in this pack are used solely to **visually identify** the software, service, game, or platform they represent, so that users of the Elgato Stream Deck can recognize and organize their shortcuts and controls.

This use falls under **nominative fair use**: referring to a trademark to identify the actual product or service it represents, without suggesting sponsorship, partnership, or official status.

No modification is made to third-party logos beyond, where applicable, technical adaptations (such as light/dark variants) intended purely for visual consistency within the pack, without altering brand identity.
